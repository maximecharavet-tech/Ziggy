# Mode dodo

`/dodo` — 31 bedtime stories told by Ziggy: **Princesse Lili et le royaume des mille secrets**
(official visual in `public/dodo/lili`) and 30 original stories, each built around one universal value
(kindness, sharing, honesty, courage, patience, friendship, respect, gratitude, perseverance,
forgiveness, empathy, nature, teamwork, self-confidence, difference, responsibility, generosity,
listening, calm, curiosity, humility, family, helping, trust). 6 scenes each, French and English,
checked by ChildShield (`src/data/dodo/dodo.test.ts`).

## Player
Cover → story (slow Ken Burns illustrations or an animated night scene, highlighted subtitles,
pause / scenes / text / volume) → moral + "Bonne nuit" → breathing balloon → sleep screen.
The screen dims slowly; a wake lock keeps it on during the story only.

## Sleep sounds — Hyper Frequencies
Ported from Hyper Engine's Frequencies app (`src/lib/dodo/sound`): rain, waves, music box (A = 432 Hz
pentatonic), cocoon (brown noise), 432 Hz lullaby, and the "Endormissement" program (binaural beat
gliding 10 → 2 Hz, headphones, very low). Tones are capped at a low level for children and duck under
Ziggy's voice. Timer: the sounds keep going 0–45 min after the story, then fade out. No health claims.

## Illustrations and voice: generated once
Pre-generated by the owner from `/dodo` (panel at the bottom, owner account only):
- **Illustrations**: NVIDIA NIM FLUX.1-schnell (`NVIDIA_API_KEY`), or Agnes (`AGNES_API_KEY`).
  Princesse Lili's scenes use her visual as a reference (FLUX Kontext / Agnes edit) so she stays the same.
- **Voice**: Gemini TTS, Ziggy's voice in a soft "bedtime" style (`GEMINI_API_KEY`), one clip per scene
  plus the outro, French by default (English optional).
Files go to the public Supabase bucket `ziggy-dodo`, listed in `ziggy_dodo_assets` (public read, owner
write — migration `20261011000000_ziggy_dodo.sql`). Until a file exists, the player uses the animated
scene and the live voice (Gemini, else the browser voice), so every story works from day one.
