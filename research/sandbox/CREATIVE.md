# Bac à sable magique: creative direction

Based on `/home/user/Ziggy/src/games2d/sandbox/content.ts`, `scenes.ts` and the game-design skill; no web search used, nothing modified. ★ = top-12 delight element, **W** = wow moment, →n = chains into reaction n.

## 1. Eight worlds
- **Garden**: fresh green-gold morning sun. Signature: rain, then a rainbow and a whole meadow blooming. Critter: a snail that surfs trickles and sneezes pollen.
- **Mountain**: lilac-blue with pink alpenglow. Signature: snowmelt becomes a waterfall. Critter: a marmot that whistles and rolls downhill as a snowball.
- **Islands**: turquoise and coral under sparkling noon light. Signature: the tide fills a castle moat. Critter: a crab that wears whatever falls on it as a hat.
- **Volcano**: plum night lit by honey-orange lava (jam, not fire). Signature: lava meets sea and an island is born. Critter: "Bloup", a lava blob that burps heart-shaped smoke rings.
- **Undersea**: teal fading to indigo, with god-rays. Signature: lights dim and plankton glows under the finger. Critter: a pufferfish that inflates when bubbles tickle it.
- **Space**: indigo-magenta nebula, no "down". Signature: dust clumps into a ringed planet. Critter: a tiny saucer alien that hiccups stars.
- **Moon**: silver with blue Earthlight, everything in slow motion. Signature: prints that stay forever. Critter: a moon bunny with enormous "boing" jumps.
- **Mars**: butterscotch sky, blue sunset. Signature: the first sprout under a dome. Critter: a googly-eyed rover that says "bip?" and hoards pebbles.

## 4. Creation beyond painting (in priority order)
1. **Critter stamps**: the residents react to elements (the crab digs, the snail drinks, Bloup naps in lava). They are never harmed; they hop away.
2. **Time dial**: pause, slow motion and a day/night toggle. Night reveals glow. No clock.
3. **Photo and sticker album**: snapshots stay on the device, with stamps of unlocked critters.
4. **Building blocks**: glass, dome, pipe, sluice gate and seesaw.
5. **Weather wand**: rain, breeze, sun angle, tide, and a gravity slider (ages 7+).
6. **Music**: each element has a pitch family (crystal chimes, drip marimba, geyser drum), so falling cells play a soft generative tune.
7. **Invitations**: "Et si… ? / What if…?" cards from the critter. They are ignorable and have no score or completion bar.

## 5. Visual and sound direction
- **Cells**: hue jitter per cell, a soft top-left highlight and a darker underside. Render at 2–3× with smoothing only on liquids, so they look like rounded blobs.
- **Liquids**: a bright surface line, depth gradient, caustics on the floor and foam where they fall.
- **Glow**: an additive bloom buffer for lava, plankton, stars and crystal. Light sources softly tint neighbouring cells.
- **Faces**: occasional blinking eyes on large blobs (a lava puddle, a cloud, a planet). They are always smiling or surprised and limited to about 5 on screen.
- **Backgrounds**: 3-layer painted parallax per world, with the sky reacting to the scene (steam fogs it, night falls).
- **Particles**: sparkles on every discovery, splash droplets and heat shimmer. All are transform/opacity only and switched off under reduced motion.
- **Sound**: a soft granular bed per element, with pitch following flow rate. Each discovery plays a 3-note motif plus the read-aloud line, and each world has its own ambience. Nothing sudden or loud.

## 6. Cut, avoid and stage
- **No scary fire**: no flames, burning, explosions, acid, lightning strikes, black holes or meteors-as-threat. Lava is warm and sleepy.
- **No harm or destruction framing**: nothing is "destroyed" (it "changes"), critters cannot be buried or hurt, and plants "go back to sleep as seeds".
- **Ages 4–6**: 6 big tools per world (64 px), one brush size, critters and the album. No sliders.
- **Ages 7–9**: full palette in a second drawer, the time dial, the weather wand and the notebook chains.
- **Ages 10–12**: gravity and temperature sliders, building blocks, music mode and "explain why" notebook pages.
- **Ship order**: Volcano and Undersea first (highest density of wow moments), then Space and Moon (they need the gravity engine), then Mars.
